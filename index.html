Frequently Asked Questions

A few things customers usually want to know.

Quick answers about choosing, installing and using an ARVIQUE retractable clothesline.

What is the difference between Single Rope and Double Rope? +
Single Rope models provide one retractable drying line and suit compact spaces. Double Rope models provide two parallel drying lines, giving you more hanging space within one wall-mounted system.
Should I choose 4.2M or 5.2M? +
Choose according to the wall-to-wall distance you need to cover. The 4.2M models are suited to shorter spans, while the 5.2M models provide additional reach for larger spaces.
Where can an ARVIQUE retractable clothesline be used? +
Depending on the model and installation surface, ARVIQUE retractable clotheslines are designed for spaces such as balconies, bathrooms, laundry areas, utility spaces and other suitable indoor or outdoor locations.
Does the clothesline stay visible when I am not using it? +
The line retracts back into the wall-mounted housing after use, helping keep the space open and visually uncluttered.
Do all ARVIQUE models have the same installation method? +
No. Installation details can vary by model and wall surface. Always follow the instructions supplied with your specific product and review the relevant product page before installation.
How do I register my product warranty? +
Use the warranty registration page on the ARVIQUE website and submit the requested purchase and product information. You can also reach ARVIQUE Care if you need assistance.
