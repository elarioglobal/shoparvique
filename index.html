About ARVIQUE

A modern home & living brand from India.

ARVIQUE is a home and living products brand by Elario Global, headquartered in Chandigarh, India. We focus on practical, space-conscious products that combine everyday functionality with clean modern design.

Our current range includes wall-mounted retractable clotheslines for balconies, bathrooms, utility areas and compact laundry spaces, with more thoughtfully designed home essentials planned as the brand grows.
