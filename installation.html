Installation Sequence

The setup in four clear stages.

The exact mounting method can differ by product and wall surface, so always follow the instructions supplied with your specific ARVIQUE model.

1

Choose the location

Select suitable opposing surfaces with enough distance for the line and enough clearance for laundry.

2

Secure the unit

Mount the main body and receiving point using the method specified for your product and wall surface.

3

Extend & connect

Pull the line to the required distance and connect it securely to the receiving point.

4

Lock & tension

Set the lock/tension controls correctly before placing laundry on the clothesline.

Before You Install

Wall suitability matters.

A retractable clothesline places tension on its mounting points. Correct installation and a suitable surface are essential for reliable use.

✓

Use the installation method specified for your exact model.

✓

Ensure the receiving point and main body are securely fixed.

✓

Do not rely on an unsuitable, loose or damaged wall surface.

✓

Check line tension and mounting stability before adding laundry.
