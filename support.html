Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions, answered.

For product-specific questions not covered here, contact ARVIQUE Care directly.

Where can I register my ARVIQUE warranty?

You can register your warranty using the official ARVIQUE warranty form linked throughout this support page.

I need help installing my product. What should I do?

Email ARVIQUE Care with your product name and a short description of the installation issue. Photos can also help explain the situation clearly.

What information should I include when contacting support?

Include your product name, purchase/order details where relevant, and a clear description of the issue or question so the support request can be understood quickly.

I purchased through Amazon. Where should I go for order or delivery issues?

For marketplace order, payment, delivery or return-process issues, Amazon may also need to assist because the order was placed through its platform. ARVIQUE Care can still help with product-related questions.

Can I contact ARVIQUE for product-use questions after purchase?

Yes. ARVIQUE Care is available for installation guidance, product-use questions and warranty-related support.
