Privacy Policy

How ARVIQUE handles information submitted through warranty registration, customer support and this website.

Last updated: September 2026ARVIQUE • Elario Global
Important: This policy describes the information ARVIQUE may receive through its website, support email and warranty-registration form. Where a third-party service such as Google Forms or Amazon is used, that service may also process information under its own privacy terms.

1. Information We May Collect

When you contact ARVIQUE, register a warranty, or otherwise submit information voluntarily, we may receive details such as your name, email address, phone number, product information, order or purchase details, and the content of your support request.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • process warranty registrations;
  • respond to customer-support and installation queries;
  • verify relevant product or purchase details where needed;
  • improve product guidance, service quality and customer experience; and
  • communicate with you about a support request you initiated.

3. Google Forms and Other Third-Party Services

ARVIQUE currently uses Google Forms for warranty registration. Information entered into that form is submitted through Google's service. Purchases may also be completed through third-party marketplaces such as Amazon.in. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

4. Cookies and Analytics

The current ARVIQUE website may use basic technical functionality required by the hosting or browser environment. If analytics, advertising or additional cookies are introduced in the future, this policy may be updated to reflect those services.

5. Sharing of Information

ARVIQUE does not intend to sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers or platforms where reasonably required to operate support, warranty registration, hosting, communications or order-related services, or where disclosure is required by law.

6. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for customer support, warranty administration, record-keeping, legal obligations and legitimate business purposes.

7. Data Accuracy and Requests

If you believe information submitted to ARVIQUE is inaccurate, or you have a privacy-related request, contact us at elarioglobal@gmail.com. Requests will be considered subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

8. Security

Reasonable efforts are made to protect information handled by ARVIQUE. However, no online transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

The ARVIQUE website and its products are intended for general consumer use and are not directed specifically to children for the purpose of collecting personal information.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version published on this website will apply from its stated update date.

11. Contact

Privacy-related questions can be sent to elarioglobal@gmail.com.
