Important: This page provides the general ARVIQUE warranty framework. The warranty period and any product-specific coverage offered to a customer may depend on the product, purchase date, promotion, insert card or other written terms supplied with that product.

1. Warranty Registration

Register Your Warranty

2. Proof of Purchase

Warranty assistance may require valid purchase information, such as an Amazon order number, invoice, purchase date, product details, photographs or other information reasonably needed to identify the product and assess the request.

3. What the Warranty Generally Covers

Where applicable, warranty coverage is intended for eligible manufacturing or material defects arising during normal household use within the stated warranty period, subject to the relevant product terms.

4. What Is Generally Not Covered

Unless expressly stated otherwise, warranty coverage does not normally extend to:

  • damage caused by incorrect installation, unsuitable surfaces or improper mounting;
  • misuse, abuse, accidental damage or use outside the product's intended purpose;
  • normal wear and tear, cosmetic changes or deterioration from ordinary use;
  • damage caused by modification, unauthorized repair or alteration;
  • damage caused by overloading or failure to follow supplied instructions; or
  • issues arising from external conditions that are not attributable to a product defect.

5. Installation Responsibility

Customers are responsible for ensuring that the installation surface and mounting method are suitable. No-drill or adhesive installation, where supplied, must be carried out in accordance with the product instructions and surface requirements. Where screw mounting is used, appropriate wall anchors and installation techniques should be selected for the surface.

6. How to Request Warranty Support

Email elarioglobal@gmail.com with your product name, purchase details, a description of the issue and relevant photographs or video where helpful.

7. Assessment and Resolution

ARVIQUE may request additional information to assess a warranty request. Where a valid warranty claim is accepted, the appropriate resolution may depend on the nature of the issue, product availability and applicable consumer law.

8. Marketplace Returns and Refunds

Orders purchased through Amazon.in are also subject to Amazon's applicable return, refund and replacement processes. Marketplace return eligibility is separate from ARVIQUE's product-support and warranty assessment.

9. Consumer Rights

Nothing in this policy is intended to exclude rights that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable consumer-protection law.
